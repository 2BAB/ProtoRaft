# Atomic wireframe prompt recipe

ProtoRaft creates one image for one screen or state. Flow arrows, labels, and relationships belong in the map layer.

## Shared prompt

Create one standalone, medium-fidelity grayscale mobile wireframe for the fictional editorial app Morrow. Show a single straight-on iPhone screen on a plain white canvas. Use precise spacing, readable neutral placeholder copy, clear information hierarchy, simple gray image blocks, thin dividers, and standard mobile controls. Keep it suitable for a product requirements document. No collage, no second screen, no arrows, no callouts, no perspective, no gradients, no shadows, no color, no photos, no decorative illustration, no visual style treatment, and no device hand.

## Screen-specific additions

- **Today:** Daily magazine home with a Morrow masthead, date, topic filters, one lead story, a short ordered briefing, and three-item bottom navigation.
- **Story:** Article detail with publication header, title, deck, byline, hero placeholder, action row for save, listen, and focus, then opening paragraphs.
- **Focus reading:** Reduced article reader with close control, reading progress, typography control, generous text measure, and no bottom navigation.
- **Now Playing:** Full audio player with story title, cover placeholder, progress slider, primary playback controls, speed control, queue access, and link to article.
- **Saved:** Personal library with filter control, in-progress section, saved story rows, progress indicators, and three-item bottom navigation.

## Acceptance checklist

- Exactly one screen is visible.
- The output can be cropped and reused independently.
- Text is legible enough to communicate hierarchy but not treated as final copy.
- No flow connector is baked into the image.
- The screen structure matches its product purpose.
